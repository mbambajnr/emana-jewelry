# 1. Executive Summary

Emana Jewelry is a luxury online jewelry platform that enables customers to:

1. Purchase ready-made jewelry products.
2. Request custom jewelry designs.
3. Source raw gemstones and precious metals.
4. Track custom production progress.
5. Consult with jewelry experts.
6. Request quotations for bespoke pieces.
7.We also do jewelry cleaning and repair services.
You can book an appointment for jewelry cleaning and repair services.
The platform aims to become a trusted destination for high-quality jewelry, custom craftsmanship, and ethical sourcing of precious materials.
